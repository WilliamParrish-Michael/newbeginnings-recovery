Wellness Services

Whole-person healing in recovery

Recovery isn’t only about breaking free from drugs or alcohol — it’s about learning to live fully again. Our wellness services are optional and supplemental, offered alongside medical and therapeutic care.

Nutrition counseling: rebuilding from within

Substance use often leaves the body starved of what it needs most. With the guidance of a registered dietitian, clients can begin to restore what addiction has taken away.

Stability

Balanced meals regulate blood sugar, reducing mood swings and cravings.

Healing

Nutrients strengthen the liver, heart, and digestive system, repairing damage from substance use.

Resilience

Better sleep, stronger immunity, and restored energy help clients engage more deeply in therapy.

Hope

Small wins — improved appetite, energy, and mood — remind clients that change is possible.

Ayurveda & mind-body practices

Our founder’s passion for Ayurveda and holistic healing shapes the spirit of New Beginnings. These services are not part of our licensed treatment program; clients who wish to explore them may do so as optional supports.

  • Yoga-inspired movement to restore calm and connection with the body.
  • Meditation and mindfulness to reduce stress and cravings.
  • Breathwork and reflection to quiet the nervous system.
  • Lifestyle balance strategies that help rebuild everyday structure.

These practices don’t replace therapy or medicine — they enhance recovery by helping clients feel more centered, resilient, and whole.

The Rancho Mirage setting

Wellness is also about environment. The desert sun, mountain views, and private courtyards of our Rancho Mirage property create a natural sense of peace. The pool offers a place for renewal, while quiet spaces allow reflection and calm.

FAQs about wellness services

What wellness services do you offer?

A range of optional, supplemental services — including mindfulness practice, yoga-inspired movement, nutritional counseling, Ayurveda, and stress-management support.

Are wellness services included in treatment programs?

Yes — wellness services are integrated into detox and residential treatment plans, complementing clinical care with physical and emotional support.

Do you offer personalized wellness plans?

Yes — our clinical and wellness teams collaborate to tailor wellness plans to each client’s needs, supporting physical, emotional, and mental health.

Begin whole-person healing today

Every client receives safe, evidence-based care, with the option to explore services that strengthen body, mind, and spirit.
