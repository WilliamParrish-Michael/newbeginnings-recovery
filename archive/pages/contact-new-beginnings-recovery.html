What to Expect When You Call

Making the call can feel overwhelming, but we’ve simplified the process:

  1. Compassionate Conversation — We listen first. You’ll never feel judged or rushed.
  2. Confidential Assessment — We’ll ask a few questions about your situation so we can guide you toward the right level of care.
  3. Insurance & Payment Review — Our staff can quickly verify insurance benefits and explain options.
  4. Next Steps — If New Beginnings is the right fit, we’ll help you with admissions and arrival details immediately.

Families Are Welcome to Call

Many of our calls come from parents, spouses, and loved ones. Even if your loved one is not ready, we will:

  • Provide resources and guidance for families.
  • Explain how our programs work.
  • Encourage ways to begin the conversation about treatment.
