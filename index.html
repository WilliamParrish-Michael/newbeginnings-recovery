How we fit into the Coachella Valley

Close enough to reach. Far enough to heal.

Rancho Mirage sits minutes from Palm Springs and the wider Coachella Valley — accessible for families, yet quiet and private. Our residential setting is intentionally unlike a hospital: warm, calm, and built for the work of getting well.

Substance use and alcohol dependence remain among the most pressing health challenges in California. We meet people where they are, with clinical care that’s honest about the road and steady all the way through it.
